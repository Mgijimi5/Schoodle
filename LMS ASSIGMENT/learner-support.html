🤝 Learner Support Centre

Schoodle provides support to help learners succeed academically, emotionally, physically and spiritually.

  • Access lesson notes and learning resources.
  • Attend online consultations with lecturers.
  • Join study groups and discussion forums.
  • Submit assignments online.
  • Receive feedback on assessments.

  • Student counselling services.
  • Stress and anxiety management workshops.
  • Mental health awareness programmes.
  • Peer support groups.

  • Campus clinic information.
  • Sports and recreation activities.
  • Healthy lifestyle programmes.
  • Fitness and wellness initiatives.

  • Chaplaincy services.
  • Prayer and meditation spaces.
  • Faith-based student organisations.
  • Guidance and spiritual counselling.
