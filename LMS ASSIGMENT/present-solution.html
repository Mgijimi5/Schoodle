📚 Topic Overview

This topic focuses on presenting information clearly and professionally. Learners will explore different methods of communicating solutions using documents, presentations, charts and other digital media while considering the intended audience.

🎯 Learning Outcomes
  • Create professional digital documents.
  • Present information using appropriate presentation tools.
  • Use charts, tables and images effectively.
  • Communicate information clearly for different audiences.
  • Evaluate and improve the presentation of digital solutions.
