📂 TOPIC 2

Process Data and Information

Duration: Week 4 - Week 6

0%
📚 Topic Overview

This topic focuses on processing data into meaningful information using digital tools. Learners will explore techniques for capturing, organising, analysing and interpreting data to support decision-making and solve real-world problems.

🎯 Learning Outcomes
  • Differentiate between data and information.
  • Capture and organise data accurately.
  • Use digital tools to process information efficiently.
  • Analyse and interpret processed information.
  • Present accurate information to support decision-making.
